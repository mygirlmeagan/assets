Step 2: Paste it into your email

Gmail Outlook Apple Mail (Mac) iPhone Android

Gmail

  1. Open Gmail on your computer and click the gear icon in the top right, then See all settings.
  2. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to Signature.
  3. If you have an old signature there, click it and delete everything in the box. If not, click Create new and name it something like "Main."
  4. Click inside the box and paste (Cmd + V or Ctrl + V).
  5. Under Signature defaults, choose your new signature for both new emails and replies.
  6. Scroll all the way down and click Save Changes.

Outlook

Outlook on the web or the new Outlook app

  1. Click the gear icon in the top right, then go to Account and Signatures.
  2. Click New signature, give it a name, and paste into the box.
  3. Under Select default signatures, pick it for new messages and for replies/forwards.
  4. Click Save.

Classic Outlook on a PC

  1. Go to File, then Options, then Mail, then the Signatures button.
  2. Click New, name it, and paste into the edit box.
  3. On the right, choose it for new messages and replies/forwards, then click OK.

Apple Mail on a Mac

  1. Open Mail and go to Mail in the top menu, then Settings (or Preferences), then Signatures.
  2. Click your email account in the left column, then click the + button.
  3. Uncheck Always match my default message font.
  4. Delete the placeholder text in the right-hand box and paste your signature.
  5. Close the window. It saves on its own.

iPhone

Your phone keeps its own signature, separate from your computer, so you'll set this one up too.

  1. On your iPhone, open this page in Safari and tap Copy my signature at the top.
  2. Open Settings, then Apps, then Mail, then Signature. (On older iPhones it's Settings, then Mail, then Signature.)
  3. Tap Per Account, delete "Sent from my iPhone" under your work email, and paste.

If it pastes as plain text with no colors or pictures, shake your phone right away and tap Undo. That brings the formatted version back. The phone version may look a little simpler than on your computer, and that's normal.

Android

Gmail app

Set up your signature in Gmail on your computer first (see the Gmail steps above). The Gmail app on Android will use that same signature as long as your phone doesn't have its own.

  1. Open the Gmail app and tap the menu (three lines) in the top left, then Settings.
  2. Tap your work email address.
  3. Find Mobile signature. If there's anything in it, delete it and save, or turn it off.
  4. Send yourself a test email from your phone to make sure your full signature shows up.

If the app still doesn't show your full signature, update the Gmail app in the Play Store and try again. Some phones are still getting this feature.
